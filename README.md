# రామ్ నారాయణ బ్రాహ్మణ సేవా సంస్థ
### Ram Narayana Brahmana Seva Samstha - Dilsukhnagar, Hyderabad

A modern, responsive, and bilingual (Telugu / English) website for **Ram Narayana Brahmana Seva Samstha**, guided by **Pro. Namburi Adinarayana Murthy**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Vedic & Golden Aesthetic:** Rich temple maroon and pure gold Vedic aesthetics with authentic typography.
- **Bilingual Support (Telugu & English):** Real-time language switcher for all content, services, mantras, and credentials.
- **Core Vedic Ancestral Services (4 Key Categories):**
  1. వైదిక బ్రాహ్మణుల సేవా (Vaidika Brahmana Seva)
  2. భోక్తల సేవా (Bhoktha Seva)
  3. పితృకార్యాలు & శ్రాద్ధకర్మలు (Pitru Karyas & Shraddha Karmas - Abdikam, Masikam, Samvatsarikam, Tarpanam)
  4. సాత్విక వంట & సంపూర్ణ నిర్వహణ (Satvik Catering & Full Arrangements)
- **Spiritual Significance & Benefits of Shraddha:** Detailed breakdown of filial duty (Pitru Rina) and Shradha significance.
- **Why Choose Us:** Clear authenticity guarantees, Agnihotra parayanam, and gotra/tithi traditional guidance.
- **Media & Credentials Gallery:** Lightbox view for official banners, visiting cards, and downloadable brochures.
- **Interactive Location & Instant Contact:** One-tap calling, WhatsApp inquiries, and embedded Google Maps for the Dilsukhnagar Samstha.
- **100% Responsive Design:** Optimized for Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens with iOS safe-area support.
