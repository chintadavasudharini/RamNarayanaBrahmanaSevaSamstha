# రామ్ నారాయణ బ్రాహ్మణ సేవ సంస్థ (మఠం)
### Ram Narayana Brahmana Seva Samstha (Matham) - Dilsukhnagar, Hyderabad

A modern, responsive, and bilingual (Telugu / English) website for **Ram Narayana Brahmana Seva Samstha / Matham**, guided by **Pro. Namburi Adinarayana Murthy**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Vedic & Golden Aesthetic:** Rich temple maroon and pure gold Vedic aesthetics with authentic typography.
- **Bilingual Support (Telugu & English):** Real-time language switcher for all content, services, mantras, and credentials.
- **Service Showcases (6 Key Categories):** Detailed Vedic services including Vaidika Brahmana Seva, Purohita Seva, Bhoktha Seva, Pitru Karyas & Shradha Karma, Shubhakaryas & Vivaham, and Satvik Catering & Full Arrangements.
- **Spiritual Benefits & Why Choose Us:** Clear breakdown of Shradha significance and authenticity guarantees.
- **Media & Credentials Gallery:** Lightbox view for official banners, visiting cards, and downloadable brochures.
- **Interactive Location & Instant Contact:** One-tap calling, WhatsApp inquiries, and embedded Google Maps for the Dilsukhnagar Matham.
- **100% Responsive Design:** Optimized for Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens with iOS safe-area support.
